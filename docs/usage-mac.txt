Overtime (macOS)
================

A tiny always-on-top desktop widget: the current time, plus how long you've
been recording. It floats over whatever you're capturing so viewers get to
watch the minutes burn -- because the "10-minute" screencast always runs long.

Nothing to install. Where it's from:
  https://github.com/jbaker-code/overtime


Launch it
---------
1. Unzip this folder (if you haven't already).
2. Put "Overtime.app" wherever you like (Applications is fine).
3. Double-click it.

It's unsigned, on purpose. If macOS balks ("unidentified developer"):
  - Right-click the app -> Open, then confirm.
  - On macOS 15+ that no longer bypasses Gatekeeper: go to
    System Settings -> Privacy & Security -> "Open Anyway".


It lives in the menu bar (no Dock icon)
---------------------------------------
Overtime is an accessory app: it has NO Dock icon and no app-switcher entry.
Instead it puts a little icon up in the menu bar (top-right of the screen,
near the system clock/battery -- look for a timer glyph). That's your remote
control for the widget.

Click the menu-bar icon to bring the widget to the front and open its menu:

  Play / Pause / Reset   drive the stopwatch without hovering the widget
  Show clock             checkbox: show/hide the wall-clock readout
  Show stopwatch         checkbox: show/hide the stopwatch readout
                         (turn both off for a blank pill)
  Settings...            open the settings panel (brings the widget forward)
  Show / Bring to Front  raise the widget if it's hidden behind something
  Float on top           checkbox: keep the widget above everything (on by
                         default). This is where you toggle always-on-top.
  Quit                   quit the app

If you ever "lose" the widget behind another window, the menu-bar icon ->
Show / Bring to Front gets it back.


Using it
--------
The widget is just the two readouts until you hover it. Hover, and a little
control strip fades in over the pill:

  |>  Play      start the stopwatch (or resume a paused one)
  ||  Pause     freeze it, keep the value; Play resumes
  <-  Reset     back to 00:00:00
  (*) Settings  open the panel to change the look (gear icon)
  x   Quit      closes the app

(There's no separate Stop button -- Pause already halts the stopwatch and
keeps the value on screen.)

  - Drag anywhere on the pill to move the widget around.
  - Resize it freely by dragging any edge or corner (or the little grip in
    the bottom-right). No locked shape -- the two readouts scale to fill
    whatever size you give it, down to a 100x60 minimum.
  - It remembers its position, size, and the stopwatch value between launches.
  - Cmd+Q also quits when the widget is focused (so does the menu bar's Quit).

Change the look: click the gear (*) in the hover controls to open the
settings panel -- tabs for Layout, Font, and Colors, all click-to-select.
The Colors tab also has a transparency slider (the pill is solid by default;
slide it down to see through to whatever's behind it). Your choices save
instantly. The window grows while the panel is open so there's room to work,
and shrinks back to your widget size when you close it.

A couple of extras -- 12/24-hour time and showing seconds -- don't have panel
tiles yet; set those by editing the config file:
  ~/Library/Application Support/Overtime/config.json
(Always-on-top lives in the menu-bar menu as "Float on top.") The config file
is safe to delete; the app just starts fresh.


Recording it in OBS
-------------------
Use Display Capture (or add the widget as its own Window Capture source). A
Window Capture of the app you're recording won't include this overlay.
